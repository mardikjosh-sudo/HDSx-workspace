# Plain-Language Summary

Among adults age 20-80 in the prepared classroom NHANES equity dataset, the mean BMI was 29.4 kg/m2 for the low-income group, 29.3 kg/m2 for the middle-income group, and 28.6 kg/m2 for the high-income group.

The low- and middle-income groups had nearly the same mean BMI, while the high-income group had a mean BMI 0.7-0.8 kg/m2 lower. This is only a descriptive comparison in the supplied classroom data.

This summary does not show that income causes differences in BMI and cannot be treated as a population estimate. A survey-weighted, covariate-adjusted analysis could help determine whether the pattern persists under a more appropriate design.
